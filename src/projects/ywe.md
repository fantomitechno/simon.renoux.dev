The project You Will Eat (YWE) was created by our team of 12 students for the "Conference PEIP" for my second year of Preparotary Class.

The idea was to create an application helping you choose menus depending of your physical activity. (calculated via a connected watch)

The project was working with a custom built watch, a web server coupled to a database hosted for 1 year (the duration of the project, disconnected since) and the application.
