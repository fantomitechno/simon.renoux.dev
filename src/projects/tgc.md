The Golden Challenge is a dumb idea I got from a stream at 2AM.

The concept is that you play a Celeste map and when arriving at the end, you're asked to connect to a Minecraft server and do a specific objective.

This got a bit of attention (one of the biggest Celeste content creator made a [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7DOppfhz37c) about it)

I'm trying to work on a new version of it where it would be a full on two players adventure: one in Celeste & one in Minecraft

It can be downloaded on [Gamebanana](https://gamebanana.com/mods/538975)
